Cogspin Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

This policy explains what information the game Cogspin (“the game”) collects, how it’s used and shared, and the choices you have. Cogspin is made by Justin Ray Chang (“we” or “us”).

Questions or requests about your privacy: hikaritrades@gmail.com

In short

  • You don’t need an account. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your progress is saved on your device.
  • The game uses Unity’s online services for its leaderboards, settings and gameplay statistics, and ad networks to show ads. These receive device and usage information, described below.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), we ask for your consent before your information is used for personalized ads or statistics, and you can change your choice at any time.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress, scores and records, coins and power-ups, settings and privacy choices on your device. It stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data. We don’t receive it.

2. Information collected through services we use

Online player profile and leaderboards (Unity Gaming Services)

When the game first goes online, Unity Technologies creates an anonymous player ID and a randomly generated player name for you. There is no sign-in, email or password. The leaderboards store your player ID, player name and scores. Your player name and scores are visible to other players on the leaderboards.

Game settings (Unity Remote Config)

To deliver the game’s settings, including tests of different settings with different groups of players, the game sends Unity your player ID, basic device information and your approximate location, worked out from your IP address. Unity states that Remote Config doesn’t store this information.

Gameplay statistics (Unity Analytics)

The game sends Unity information about how the game is played, such as levels reached, games that end, ads watched and the estimated revenue from ads. This comes with an installation ID, device information (such as model, operating system and language), approximate location, and crash and performance data. In regions where consent is required, this happens only if you agree. We use it to understand how the game is played and to improve it.

Ads (Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads and ironSource Ads)

The game shows ads, including optional ads you can choose to watch for a reward. To deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalize ads, these ad services collect your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers, approximate location (from your IP address), device and app information, how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data. Unity Ads may also collect information about purchases. This information is shared with these ad networks, which may share it with their advertising partners under their own privacy policies. If we add other ad networks, we’ll update this policy.

Your privacy choices (Google)

In regions where consent is required, the game shows a consent form provided by Google’s User Messaging Platform. Google receives the information it needs to show the form and record your choices, and your choices are saved on your device.

Purchases

If you make a purchase in the game, payment is handled by Google Play. We don’t receive your payment details, only confirmation of the purchase.

3. How we use information

  • To run the game and its online features, such as leaderboards.
  • To remember your settings and privacy choices.
  • To show ads, which keep the game free.
  • To understand how the game is played and improve it.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to comply with the law.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers described above: Unity Technologies and its affiliates (including ironSource) and Google. We don’t share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it or to protect the rights and safety of players and others.

5. Your choices

  • Privacy options: where they apply, choose Settings, then Privacy options, in the game to change your consent choices at any time.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your device’s advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device’s settings (usually under Privacy, then Ads, or Google, then Ads).
  • Deleting your data: uninstalling the game removes the data on your device. To delete the information Unity holds for your player (player ID, player name, scores and statistics), email us with your player name as shown on the leaderboards. We’ll delete it within 30 days and confirm when it’s done. Ad networks’ own data can be managed through their privacy policies, linked below.

6. How long information is kept

Information on your device stays until you delete it. Information held by Unity’s services is kept only as long as it’s needed for the purposes above, or until you ask us to delete it, under Unity’s retention practices. Ad networks keep information under their own privacy policies.

7. Security

The game sends information over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, but we and our service providers take reasonable steps to protect it.

8. Children

Cogspin is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age where you live), and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

9. Where information is processed

Our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries, which may have different data protection laws from the country where you live.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example in the European Economic Area, the UK or certain US states such as California, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to its use, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of having it used for targeted advertising. To use these rights, use the privacy options in the game or email us. We won’t treat you differently for doing so.

11. Other companies’ policies

  • Unity privacy policies, including the Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, for players of games that use Unity’s technology.
  • Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We’ll post the new version on this page with a new effective date, and for significant changes we’ll also let you know in the game.
